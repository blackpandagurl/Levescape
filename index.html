Welcome to LEVESCAPE!

Levi the cat is keeping you trapped inside. Find all 6 keys, solve the room challenges, and escape through the front door.

The Wellness Room is your safe space because Levi cannot swim. But be careful in the other rooms… especially the Red Room!

How to play: use the arrows or WASD to move, complete the challenges, and collect the keys. If Levi catches you, you’ll need to try again.
